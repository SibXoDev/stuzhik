Now0.0.20 · 2026-10

Fixing what we found

35 of 36 known issues closed — in instances, mods, modpacks, servers and Connect. And 19 new backgrounds.

In October we went through the whole launcher and confirmed more than fifty bugs. The most serious ones were fixed back in 0.0.19, and now 35 of the remaining 36 are closed: instance edits save completely, a modpack update changes everything or nothing, the whitelist works for offline players, RCON stays in step, and “Get the pack” shows real progress.

One is left — a friend vanishes if you both changed networks and share no VPN; it is on the known issues page with a workaround. Also: Crystal Ball is the new default background, 18 more backgrounds, cursor effects, Telegram links open in the app, and “What's new” got search and filters.

Highlights

  • Instances: edits save completely
  • Modpacks: an update is all or nothing
  • Server: whitelist for offline players, reliable RCON
  • Connect: honest transfer progress
  • 19 new backgrounds and cursor effects
